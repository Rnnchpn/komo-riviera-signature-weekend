KŌMØ Motion

Useful data.
Human interpretation.

The centrepiece is a guided functional movement session, followed by an individual conversation about the capacities that support the life you want to keep living.

ObserveMobility, balance, strength, coordination and confidence.

UnderstandA clear, intelligible conversation—not a score for its own sake.

ContinueA concise roadmap and optional connection to KŌMØ Pulse.

KŌMØ Motion is educational and non-diagnostic. It does not replace a medical consultation, diagnosis or treatment.
