# Before your first deploy

This project includes a live invitation endpoint. The site will render without configuration, but the invitation form will only send emails after the required Brevo variables are set.

## Required

- \`BREVO_API_KEY\`
- \`BREVO_LIST_ID\`
- \`BREVO_DOI_TEMPLATE_ID\`
- \`SITE_URL\`
- \`ALLOWED_ORIGINS\`

The complete guide is in \`README.md\`.

## Required Brevo attributes

\`FIRSTNAME\`, \`LANGUAGE\`, \`PROFILE\`, \`AREA\`, \`SOURCE\`, \`EVENT_KEY\`, \`EVENT_STATUS\`, \`CONSENT_AT\`, \`UTM_SOURCE\`, \`UTM_MEDIUM\`, \`UTM_CAMPAIGN\`, \`UTM_CONTENT\`.

## Final launch checkpoint

Complete the operator fields in \`legal.html\` before accepting public paid bookings.
